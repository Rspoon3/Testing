# Features

## Accidental Tech Podcast App

### Episode Browsing
Browse all available episodes of The Accidental Tech Podcast fetched from the iTunes API. Episodes are displayed in chronological order with title, publish date, and duration.

### Episode Downloads
Download episodes for offline playback with visual progress indicator. Downloaded episodes are stored permanently in the app's documents directory.

### Audio Playback
Play downloaded episodes with AVPlayer, featuring background audio support so playback continues when the app is backgrounded or the screen is locked.

### Playback Controls
Simple playback controls including play/pause, skip forward/backward (15 seconds), and a draggable progress bar for seeking within episodes.

### Progress Persistence
Automatically saves and restores playback position for each episode. Resume listening from where you left off, even after closing the app.

### Swipe to Delete
Delete downloaded episodes using the standard iOS swipe-to-delete gesture to free up storage space.

### Downloaded Episodes Section
Dedicated section showing all downloaded episodes with their current playback progress and completion status.
